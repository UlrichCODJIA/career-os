# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Protect private information and security reports.
- Accept correction and help make contribution paths accessible.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their verified GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
